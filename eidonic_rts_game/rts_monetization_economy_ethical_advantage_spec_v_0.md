# RTS — Monetization & Economy — Ethical Advantage Spec v0

> **North Star:** Fund wonder without wounding trust. Create *situational, transparent, counterable* advantages that reward stewardship and coordination—not oppression.

---

## 1) Principles & Safeguards
- **No predation:** visible prices, odds, and guarantees; regional compliance; frictionless spend limits & cooldowns; playtime reminders.  
- **Edge Budget:** account‑level cap on total combat edge a player can carry at once (e.g., +X% aggregate across all active buffs). Excess converts to *cosmetic flourish* or *teamwide benefits*.  
- **Situational, not permanent:** edges only apply within declared contexts (defense windows, anomaly ops, council wars) with shard‑wide cooldowns.  
- **Counters exist:** every edge has 1–2 tactical counters obtainable by F2P players via play.  
- **Transparency:** all edges labeled with power score, duration, counters, and server cooldowns; public dashboards show distribution.  
- **Anti‑oppression tripwires:** Oppression Index watchers auto‑throttle edges if bullying patterns emerge.

---

## 2) Ethical FOMO (Festivals & Cycles)
- **Festival Passes (time‑boxed, non‑mandatory):** unlock a line of *festival‑only missions* with guaranteed cosmetics + **1 situational edge** token. Reissue later as **Heritage** with palette shifts (no forever FOMO).  
- **Cycle Caches (not loot boxes):** transparent tables; players pick 1 of 3 face‑up rewards per cache. Pity counter guarantees top‑tier after N caches.  
- **Server Seeds (Patron):** fund a new shard’s launch; backers get naming rights, plaza ornaments, and **teamwide logistical edges** during the shard’s first festival (no personal DPS).  
- **World Reseeding Rituals:** limited windows where rare blueprints return; framed as lore events.

---

## 3) Commander / General Perks (Whale‑leaning, team‑first)
> **Design intent:** whales feel powerful by *leading*, not by solo steamrolling.

- **Command Bands:** Stewards unlock **Command Roles** in wars; perks only active while commanding and scale with *allied participation*.  
- **Perk Ladders (pick two at a time; rotate weekly):**  
  - **Logistics Maestro:** +supply throughput to *allied* lanes; enemy can snipe depots to suppress.  
  - **Battle Choir:** enables synchronized ally abilities; mis‑timed use wastes the window.  
  - **Siege Engineer:** faster emplacement build; counters: sabotage kits / EMP storms.  
  - **Guardian Phalanx:** shield‑wall orders reduce FF accidents; counters: flanking / artillery arcs.  
  - **Recon Nexus:** bonus fog reveal *only* via scout network; counters: jammers / decoys.  
- **Duty Meter:** perks drain a meter filled by civic acts (public works, mentorship). Abuse, farm‑stomping, or harassment freezes perks.

---

## 4) Titan Synergy (Alliance Edge)
- **Choir Synergy Bonus:** if the full choir hits QTE windows, the titan gains a short *synergy aura* that **buffs allies more than self**.  
- **Build Slots (limited):** Hull/Core/Limbs/Aura/Ultimate. Certain premium ornaments non‑combat; certain *edge modules* only active during declared *Titan Events* with published counters.  
- **Counterplay:** enemy councils can craft **Rift Shackles**, **Disruption Choirs**, or **Anima Leeches** to negate or invert titan auras.

---

## 5) Anomaly Contracts (Edge with Accountability)
- **Time‑boxed missions** purchasable by alliances or stewards that unlock *one* situational buff for a region (e.g., +harvest yield, +repair speed, +anti‑siege ward).  
- **Contracts require**: council vote, materials, and an **Atonement Bond**—misuse (bullying, griefing) consumes the bond and disables future contracts for a cycle.

---

## 6) Edge Examples (capped, counterable)
- **Siege Engine Overclock (+10% build/aim during declared sieges)**; counter: EMP storms; cap: 15m; shard cooldown: 4h.  
- **Command Resonance (+8% morale to nearby *allies* when issuing formation orders)**; counter: fear totems; cap: within 30m after order.  
- **Anomaly Insight (extra weak‑point reveals in Physics Archery Clash)**; counter: camouflage mist; cap: 3 reveals per match.  
- **Choir Echo (titan ability echo once per event)**; counter: disruption choir with perfect timing.  
- **Ward Architect (place 1 additional ward in defense windows)**; counter: wardbreakers with mini‑game.

> All edges count toward the **Edge Budget** cap; edges beyond cap convert to visuals or team buffs.

---

## 7) Responsible Chance Mechanics
- **No blind loot boxes.** All chance systems show tables and pity counters *before purchase*.  
- **Region‑aware compliance:** where chance monetization is restricted, we replace with selection tokens or direct purchase.  
- **Session Protections:** soft cooldowns after heavy spend; optional monthly caps; full refund grace within short windows for minors (child shards won’t surface monetization).

---

## 8) Revenue Streams (diversified)
- Patron Spectacle (city auroras, processions, music)  
- Festival Passes & Cycle Caches (transparent, guaranteed)  
- Creator Forge royalties (UGC)  
- Alliance Titan ornaments (cosmetic)  
- Ethical Anomaly Contracts (with Bonds)  
- Server Seeds (logistics‑only edges)  
- Cosmetics (earnable + purchase)

---

## 9) Telemetry & Ethics Dashboards
- **Edge Distribution Heatmap:** who uses what, where, and against whom.  
- **Oppression Index:** auto‑throttles edge issuance if bullying patterns surface.  
- **Spend Health:** average spend, cap adherence, refund rates.  
- **Proceeds & Impact:** public works funded, charities delivered, creator royalties paid.
